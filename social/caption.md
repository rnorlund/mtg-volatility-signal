## Short post

Most price talk is about direction: is this card going up or down? The question nobody asks is how FAR it could move. A card that drifts 5% and a card that doubles or halves are not the same bet, even if you are equally sure about the direction.

So I built a model that scores every Magic card 0 to 100 on volatility, which is how violently its price swings right now. It is measured straight from each card's own price history: how big its weekly moves are, its worst drawdown, and how prone it is to sudden jumps. For every card it also gives an expected price range over the next 3, 6, and 12 months.

The results pass the gut check. Heavily reprinted, deeply played staples like Cyclonic Rift and The One Ring barely move week to week, so they score low. Cards rocked by bans and buyouts, like Dockside Extortionist and Mana Crypt, and freshly hyped specials swing wildly, so they score high.

The punchline: volatility is not the same as risk of loss, it is the SIZE of the move in either direction. Knowing it is what lets you size a position properly, bigger on the steady cards, smaller on the wild ones.

What is the most volatile card in your collection, the one whose price you can never predict? 👀

## Posting tips

- Lead with the idea ("how far, not just which way"), not the math.
- Frame it as a fun tool, not financial advice.
- Have a couple of surprising examples ready (steady expensive staple vs wild mover) for the comments.
- Check the group's self-promo rules first.
